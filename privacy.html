Privacy

How Nick Autoservice uses the details you send through this website. Last updated 26 November 2026.

Who we are

Nick Autoservice, 19 Zone A, Mullingar Business Park, Mullingar, Co. Westmeath, is responsible for the details you send us. For anything about your data, ring 089 477 0532 or email nicumereacre53@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into the forms on this site, and nothing until you press send:

  • Book in: your name, phone number, reg and what the car needs, plus the make and model, a preferred date, your email and a note if you add them.
  • Sell your car: your name, phone number, reg and mileage, plus the make and model, its condition, whether you are trading in, your email and notes if you add them.
  • Ask about a car: your name, phone number and which car, plus your email and your question if you add them.

What we use it for

To ring or message you back, arrange the booking, value your car or answer your question. Under data protection law this is taking the steps you asked for before a job or a sale. If the work or the sale goes ahead, your name and the car's details go on the job record and invoice, as for any customer.

We don't use your details for marketing, and we never sell them or pass them on, except to the services below that deliver your message.

How your message reaches Nick

When you press send, the form is sent through the website's host, Vercel, and Resend, an email delivery service, to Nick's Gmail inbox, which Google runs. If you choose Send on WhatsApp instead, the message goes from your own WhatsApp, which Meta runs, under WhatsApp's terms. These services handle your message only to deliver and store it, and some of them may store data outside the EU under their own data protection terms.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't lead to a job or a sale are deleted within 12 months. Where work is done or a car is sold, the invoice and job record are kept for six years, because Irish tax law requires business records to be kept that long.

Your rights

You can ask to see the details we hold about you, have them corrected or deleted, limit how we use them or object to it. Ring or email Nick and he will deal with it within a month. If you are unhappy with how we have handled your details, you can complain to the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

This website

The site sets no cookies of its own and does not track you for advertising. It counts visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which works without cookies. The map in Find us is Google Maps: when it loads, Google receives your IP address and may set its own cookies, under Google's privacy policy.
